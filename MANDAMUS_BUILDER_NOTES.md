# Mandamus Builder Addition

This build adds a new **Mandamus Builder** tab based on the uploaded USCIS Mandamus Complaint & Demand Letter Guide.

## Included workflow

- Structured USCIS delay case intake
- Petitioner and beneficiary information
- I-130/I-485/form and receipt data
- Filing date and automatic delay calculation
- Days pending and days beyond 180-day benchmark
- USCIS processing-time and Get Inquiry Date fields
- Hardship narrative collection
- Administrative contact/exhaustion timeline
- Delay-focused exhibit manager
- Demand letter generation
- Mandamus complaint generation
- Exhibit list generation
- Filing/service checklist generation
- DOCX/PDF export through the existing export route

## Safety and limitations

All generated mandamus outputs are labeled **Pending Attorney Review**. The app intentionally does not hardcode current officeholders, USCIS processing times, or Get Inquiry Date results. Staff should verify current government officials, service addresses, court rules, and USCIS data before use.

The generated complaint is a structured starter draft, not final legal advice or a final filing. It should be reviewed by an immigration attorney before sending or filing.
